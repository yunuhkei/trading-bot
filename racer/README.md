# Racer prototype

A free-roam arcade racer in **one self-contained HTML file** (`Racer_Prototype.html`, no libraries, no network).
It is built from the modules in `racer/src` by `python3 racer/build.py` (the output is committed so you can just open it).

* **Map**: one fixed county (a 10.2 km square heightmap with 5 m cells, 8.5 km of it land: the sea is to the west and south, cliffs and a beach to the east and north), roads authored in plan view only (`src/20_map.js`), heights follow the land and the land is carved around the roads:
  a ~10 km highway ring with sweeping bends (and a long straight), a mountain pass with switchbacks, a twisty coast road (Highway 1 style) with an overpass,
  a long straight on the southern plains, an east link, and the county's districts, inspired by San Francisco and California:
  **Downtown** = two street grids (one axis-aligned, one rotated to follow the diagonal **Market Street**, like SF), a tower district with a pyramid tower,
  Victorian row houses on **Nob Hill / Russian Hill** (grades over 20 percent) with a hairpin **Lombard Street** block, **Twin Peaks** with a winding road,
  **Golden Gate Park** (a long green strip with a lake and a park drive); **the Valley** = LA style wide palm-lined boulevards with strip malls, parking lots and stucco houses;
  **Mulholland** = a very twisty ridge road in the hills; **Santa Cruz** = a beach town on the south coast (bungalows, motels, a boardwalk with a Ferris wheel,
  lifeguard towers, umbrellas and a pier you can drive out on); **Palm Springs** = a desert resort town of wide palm boulevards (mid-century hotels round pools,
  a golf course, villas with pools).
  **The wider county** (`src/20_world.js`, terrain in `baseH`): a northern range with snow above about 360 m, a 75 m mesa in the north-east cut by a winding canyon (the canyon road follows the floor to a turnaround on the rim),
  a desert lake, a farm lake, and five small towns plus two industrial areas that fill the gaps (**Pacifica** on the coast road, **Fresno** farm town, **Barstow** desert town, **Big Pines** mountain village on a terrace in the foothills,
  the **harbour** industrial area, **Mojave Works**), each a street grid ringed by a boulevard like the districts (`makeTown`), with farms (barn, silo, house, shed, field) along the farm roads, more suburbs round the Valley and the towns,
  and 17 **safe houses** (a garage with a tall green sign, green dots on the map; behaviour comes later). New highways: the east highway to Barstow and the loop back through the refinery and the southern highway, the north highway over the mountain-pass loop to Big Pines, the farm roads.
  **Network** (one connected road system, checked by a script that reports roads lying on top of each other, dead ends and disconnected pieces): every district is ringed
  by a boulevard (`perimeter()`), every street of the district ends on it, and the highways join the boulevards: Geary and the park drive meet downtown's boulevard,
  the boulevard road runs from downtown across the hills to the ring, the Valley joins it and the ring, the coast road runs from Market Street's south-west end
  round the coast to the southern highway (past the Santa Cruz link), the east link joins the ring to the southern highway, Palm Springs joins the east link and the southern
  highway, Mulholland runs from the ring to the boulevard road and the mountain pass leaves the ring and returns to it. Branches leave a road along its side and peel away
  (`rampPts`) or end as a T on its edge (`edgePt`), so no road is built on top of another; where roads really cross (coast road over the ring) there is an overpass
  (found and built automatically, `fixCrossings`).
  Collision: there are no guardrails for now (`RAILS=false` in `src/21_terrain.js` brings back the highway, cliff and bridge rails); buildings (also the rotated ones), poles, trees and bridge piers are solid
  (with height ranges, so the deck of a bridge is not blocked by what is under it); steep banks are walls.
  Hills: the road-surface lookup picks the nearest road segment at the car's own level (never by height alone, which made the ground a staircase), every city street takes
  its height from one shared smoothed height field (so crossing streets are flush), connector roads blend to the street they join, the body height follows the ground
  closely and the ground cannot launch the car faster than the slope allows. The tests that measure this are in `racer/tests/` (see its README for how to run them and the baseline numbers the driving feel must keep). Everything is deterministic.
* **Lanes**: lane width is one constant (`LANE_W`, 3.6 m in `src/00_util.js`); `laneInfo(hw,style)` gives lanes per direction from the road width, and both the road shader (painted dashed lines, centre line / median) and the traffic lane graph use it, so the paint and the AI lanes always agree. Markings fade towards junctions.
* **Lane graph** (`src/23_lanes.js`, built once at boot in about 0.1 s, 482 lane edges, 2,377 turn links, 434 lane-km, 2 MB): one centre-line per lane per direction for every road except the patches (Lombard Street, the pier), stored in travel order. Junctions are found where a road end touches another road (T, ramp, end to end, three roads at one point) and where two roads cross at the same level; a turn is a short cubic curve from one lane to another (right turns from the outer lane, left turns from the inner lane, a lane that runs out of road may turn from any lane; no left turns across a highway median, so highways get a U-turn crossover about every 500 m; dead ends get a U-turn; a curve that would be shorter than 8 m is pushed further out). Each lane point has a comfortable speed from the road type, curvature and grade. `node racer/tests/lanecheck.js` checks it (the road directions form one strongly connected piece, so a car can never get trapped).
* **Traffic** (`src/60_traffic.js` simulation, `src/61_trafficgl.js` cars): up to 56 cars (`?tmax=N` changes it, `?traffic=0` turns it off) spawned out of sight 140-430 m from the player and removed beyond 540 m (or behind the player past 300 m); more cars in town (downtown 56, the Valley 51, towns 37, highways about 22-27, mountain roads about 19-22). Each car has a horizon (12 points, 6-10 m apart, 66-110 m ahead) along its lane and the turn it picked at the next junction (a seeded hash, so it never changes its mind); IDM car following with a speed profile from the lane speeds ahead; it gives way by comparing its rectangle with every other car's rectangle at the same moment over the next 4 s (road class and turn type decide who goes, then who is nearer; a car that has given way keeps doing so for a second; anything about to touch while still moving brakes whatever the priority); it changes lane to get into the lane of its turn, and to get past a slow car; swerves and brakes for the player; steers by pure pursuit (a kinematic bicycle). A car that has been waiting for a few seconds goes anyway, and a jam that lasts 12-22 s away from the player is dissolved. Four low-poly bodies (sedan, hatch, SUV, van, about 200 triangles each) in 12 colours with head and tail lights (brake lights light up), drawn instanced: one draw per body type (4 draws, about 5k triangles in town). Everything is deterministic (one seeded generator, fixed 60 Hz tick shared with the 120 Hz player step).
* **Crashes**: a hit is a real collision between two rectangles (mass 1.1-2.3 t, restitution .26, friction .32) with an impulse at the contact point, so a hit car spins. A light bump just slows the car; above 2.5 kJ it becomes a wreck (a free body: slides, spins, stops at walls and trees, stays up to 30 s and goes away sooner once out of sight). Every impact is recorded in `IMP` (`40_car.js`): source (0 building/tree/pole, 1 steep bank, 2 landing, 3 traffic), closing speed, **energy lost in joules**, contact point and normal, the car's zone (0 front, 1 rear, 2 left, 3 right), the other mass. The damage system reads `IMP`.
* **Damage** (`src/62_damage.js`): every impact in `IMP` adds its energy to one of five zones (front, rear, left, right, roof; a zone is destroyed by `DZ_CAP` = 650 / 520 / 420 / 420 / 320 kJ, a sedan at 24 m/s into a parked car is about 180 kJ; 14 % of a hit spills into the neighbouring zones; scrapes below 3.5 m/s or 8 kJ and landings below 11 m/s do nothing). The effects are exactly neutral (1 / 0) on an undamaged car, so the driving feel is untouched until you crash: front = less power and top speed, then engine smoke and fire; rear = nitro leak and a little power; left/right = the car pulls towards the damaged side; roof = a little top speed. Wrecked when the front or rear is gone or health is under 16 %. **Full dents**: the real vertices of the car body (6,559) near the contact are pushed in along the direction of the hit (Gaussian of the pristine position, up to 0.34 m deep, never more than 0.5 m from where they were, noise a function of position so flat-shaded edges do not tear), normals are shaken so the dent catches the light and the paint scuffs; the body buffer is re-uploaded with `bufferSubData` (105 kB, at once for a big hit, at most every 0.2 s for a run of small ones). Repair puts the pristine copy back. A small car-from-above health display (five zones + bar) sits next to the speed readout.
* **Safe houses** (`src/63_safe.js`): the garages on the map each have a glowing pad in front of the roller door (green = yours, red = locked). Park on it (under 9 m/s, 1.5 s, the pad slows you) and the car is repaired, the heat is cleared and the spot is saved. The Home Garage near the start is free and owned from the first second; the others are locked. **The money system is NOT decided**: `ECON` is a set of hooks that do nothing (`price(id)` returns null, `canBuy/buy` return false, `repairCost` is 0, `onBusted/onWrecked/onRepair/onUnlock` are empty) and `SAFE.price[id]` is null for every house but Home. Call `safeUnlock(id)` once a house has been paid for; progress is kept in `localStorage` (`racer.save`). Wrecked or busted: a 3.4 s end screen, then you respawn on the pad of the nearest garage you own, repaired and with no cops. `?safe=all` unlocks every garage for testing.
* **Cops** (`src/64_cops.js`, cars from `60_traffic.js` with body type 4): heat 1-5 (stars), earned from crimes (hitting traffic, wrecking cars, hitting cops, speeding past a cop) and kept by being seen. Difficulty presets in `COPD` (`?cops=off|medium|hard`; **Forgiving is the default**): Forgiving cops top out at 46 m/s, send 0/1/2/2/3/4 chasers at heat 0-5, only ram from heat 5, set up roadblocks (with a 6.5 m gap) at heat 5, need 5 s boxed in at under 6 m/s to bust you and lose you after 8 s out of sight. Medium (56 m/s, rams from heat 3, roadblocks from 4, bust in 3 s) and Hard (66 m/s, rams from 2, roadblocks from 3, bust in 2 s) are there but not tuned by hand. Cops spawn out of sight (patrols anywhere, chasers 150-270 m behind) and, when idle, patrol along the lane graph with lights off; a patrol that sees you commit a crime starts a chase. Chasers pick lanes greedily towards you, overtake slower cars, switch to free driving (steering at a slot beside, behind or in front of you) once close, and try to box you in; a cop that is hit too hard is wrecked. Escape: break line of sight (range 300 m) for 8 s, outrun them, or park in an owned garage. Sirens (instance flag, red/blue light bar materials and a two-tone oscillator) and the stars / bust meter on the HUD, flashing dots on the minimap.
* **Rendering**: terrain is a GPU clip-map (6 rings, one draw each) displaced from a 16-bit height texture; roads are ribbons with analytic
  lane markings; buildings/trees/barriers are merged per 128 m cell and culled by distance + frustum; sky, fog and water are shaders.
  About 40-110 draw calls and 60-90k triangles per frame (traffic adds 4 draws and up to 5k triangles). No shadows or reflections. A render-scale governor keeps frame time near 16 ms.
* **Car**: the Surge Sport mesh baked from the Close Call garage (`src/car_nitro.js`, made with `tools/`), wheels separate so they spin and steer.
* **Driving** (`HND` constants at the top of `src/40_car.js`): grip first, like the Need for Speed arcade games. Steering is limited by a tyre budget
  (about 1.4-2.3 g), so at speed full lock runs wide instead of sliding. Speed is shown in mph. Tuned like a sports car: firm brakes (60 mph to a stop in about 1.2 s) and quick turn-in.
  **Drift pedal**: a *tap* kicks the tail out (a slide of about 15-25 degrees towards the way you steer, or the side you last steered when the stick is centred) that carries on for about
  half a second after the finger lifts, then a stability assist straightens the car; the stick picks a bigger slide angle. The *longer you hold* it the harder it brakes (a light drag at first, the full 24 m/s^2
  locked-wheel brake after 1.2 s), and the slide angle the stick can ask for grows as the car slows, so a full-stick hold turns the car right round (about 170 degrees in 1.2 s at 60 mph, 2 s at 100, 2.9 s at 140).
  **Any slide is a drift**: points, nitro, tyre smoke and skid marks come from the slide angle (more than about 8 degrees at over 22 mph), not from the pedal, so a slide that carries on after a tap, after a landing or on grass counts too;
  a half-second grace keeps the combo alive across a change of direction. The DRIFT and AIR / JUMP readouts are small, count up live, stay about two seconds after the move ends, then float up and fade out.
  **Energy**: everything is per unit mass, with speed measured along the ground. Gravity pulls the car along the slope exactly (`a = -g m/(1+m^2)`, also sideways down a camber), so climbing turns speed into height
  and descending turns height into speed (coasting over a 30 m hill loses exactly `g h` plus drag; the test sums kinetic + potential + drag work to within 4 %). Brakes, the drift and bumps turn kinetic energy into heat.
  **Air**: the car follows the ground only while the ground pushes up on it. Over a crest of vertical curvature k the ground needs a downward acceleration of `k v^2`; once that is more than gravity the tyres lose the road and
  the car flies on ballistically with the velocity it had (energy conserved, only a little air drag; the engine revs freely and the nose follows the arc). It lands as soon as it meets the ground again: the part of the velocity that points into the ground is
  absorbed (that energy, half the normal speed squared, is lost: shake, camera bob and a small extra scrub above 12 m/s), the rest is kept, so a flat landing keeps all its horizontal speed, a landing on a down slope turns the fall into
  forward speed and one on an up slope loses some. Crossing a crest of height h and length L launches a car above about `sqrt(g L^2 / (2 pi^2 h))`; there are three groups of *jump hills* (6 m, 68 m crests, about 68 mph to take off,
  a 1 s / 50 m flight at 100 mph) on the ring's south straight, the southern highway (two groups) and the east link, marked JUMPS on the map. Also off any bank or bridge edge, and over real hills on the mountain roads.
  Cars stay on the road over small bumps, the body leans and dives. Nitro is filled by drifting and is a latch: one tap starts it, it runs until the tank is empty (tap again or brake to stop). Building, barrier and bank collisions, respawn.
* **Controls**: touch (drag the left half to steer; GAS and BRAKE pedals bottom right, DRIFT above brake, NITRO above gas; tap the minimap (top left) for the world map;
  AUTO = automatic gas) or keyboard (arrows/WASD, Space drift, Shift nitro, R respawn, M map, G auto-gas, F fps, P pause).

Modules, in load order: `00_util` math/noise/lane geometry, `10_gl` GL helpers and all shaders, `20_map` roads + terrain function, `20_world` the wider county,
`21_terrain` height map/carving/ground colours, `22_build` static geometry + spatial queries, `23_lanes` lane graph, `30_render` frame, `40_car` car/physics/camera/effects/impacts,
`50_ui` input/HUD/minimap/audio/quality, `60_traffic` traffic simulation, `61_trafficgl` traffic cars, `62_damage` damage + dents, `63_safe` safe houses + economy stub + wrecked/busted, `64_cops` cops and heat, `90_main` boot + loop (`worldStep` = player at 120 Hz + traffic at 60 Hz).

`tools/` documents how the car was extracted from the garage (needs the Close Call test harness; not needed to build the racer).
