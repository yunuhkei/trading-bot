# Racer prototype

A free-roam arcade racer in **one self-contained HTML file** (`Racer_Prototype.html`, no libraries, no network).
It is built from the modules in `racer/src` by `python3 racer/build.py` (the output is committed so you can just open it).

* **Map**: one fixed county (a 10.2 km square heightmap with 5 m cells, 8.5 km of it land: the sea is to the west and south, cliffs and a beach to the east and north), roads authored in plan view only (`src/20_map.js`), heights follow the land and the land is carved around the roads:
  a ~10 km highway ring with sweeping bends (and a long straight), a mountain pass with switchbacks, a twisty coast road (Highway 1 style) with an overpass,
  a long straight on the southern plains, an east link, and the county's districts, inspired by San Francisco and California:
  **Downtown** = two street grids (one axis-aligned, one rotated to follow the diagonal **Market Street**, like SF), a tower district with a pyramid tower,
  Victorian row houses on **Nob Hill / Russian Hill** (grades over 20 percent) with a hairpin **Lombard Street** block, **Twin Peaks** with a winding road,
  **Golden Gate Park** (a long green strip with a lake and a park drive); **the Valley** = LA style wide palm-lined boulevards with strip malls, parking lots and stucco houses;
  **Mulholland** = a very twisty ridge road in the hills; **Santa Cruz** = a beach town on the south coast (bungalows, motels, a boardwalk with a Ferris wheel,
  lifeguard towers, umbrellas and a pier you can drive out on); **Palm Springs** = a desert resort town of wide palm boulevards (mid-century hotels round pools,
  a golf course, villas with pools).
  **The wider county** (`src/20_world.js`, terrain in `baseH`): a northern range with snow above about 360 m, a 75 m mesa in the north-east cut by a winding canyon (the canyon road follows the floor to a turnaround on the rim),
  a desert lake, a farm lake, and five small towns plus two industrial areas that fill the gaps (**Pacifica** on the coast road, **Fresno** farm town, **Barstow** desert town, **Big Pines** mountain village on a terrace in the foothills,
  the **harbour** industrial area, **Mojave Works**), each a street grid ringed by a boulevard like the districts (`makeTown`), with farms (barn, silo, house, shed, field) along the farm roads, more suburbs round the Valley and the towns,
  and 17 **safe houses** (a garage with a tall green sign, green dots on the map; behaviour comes later). New highways: the east highway to Barstow and the loop back through the refinery and the southern highway, the north highway over the mountain-pass loop to Big Pines, the farm roads.
  **Network** (one connected road system, checked by a script that reports roads lying on top of each other, dead ends and disconnected pieces): every district is ringed
  by a boulevard (`perimeter()`), every street of the district ends on it, and the highways join the boulevards: Geary and the park drive meet downtown's boulevard,
  the boulevard road runs from downtown across the hills to the ring, the Valley joins it and the ring, the coast road runs from Market Street's south-west end
  round the coast to the southern highway (past the Santa Cruz link), the east link joins the ring to the southern highway, Palm Springs joins the east link and the southern
  highway, Mulholland runs from the ring to the boulevard road and the mountain pass leaves the ring and returns to it. Branches leave a road along its side and peel away
  (`rampPts`) or end as a T on its edge (`edgePt`), so no road is built on top of another; where roads really cross (coast road over the ring) there is an overpass
  (found and built automatically, `fixCrossings`).
  Collision: there are no guardrails for now (`RAILS=false` in `src/21_terrain.js` brings back the highway, cliff and bridge rails); buildings (also the rotated ones), poles, trees and bridge piers are solid
  (with height ranges, so the deck of a bridge is not blocked by what is under it); steep banks are walls.
  Hills: the road-surface lookup picks the nearest road segment at the car's own level (never by height alone, which made the ground a staircase), every city street takes
  its height from one shared smoothed height field (so crossing streets are flush), connector roads blend to the street they join, the body height follows the ground
  closely and the ground cannot launch the car faster than the slope allows. The tests that measure this are in `racer/tests/` (see its README for how to run them and the baseline numbers the driving feel must keep). Everything is deterministic.
* **Roads are smooth**: the height profile of every road (not the city streets, which follow a smoothed height field) is smoothed with a 28 m binomial filter after the grade limit (`vSmooth` in `src/20_map.js`, ends held exactly so roads still meet flush): the slope clamps used to leave kinks (a slope flipping between +22 % and +6 %, crests only 16 m in radius that threw the car into the air on the mountain pass). No crest tighter than 35 m remains except the designed jump hills (39 m).
* **Lanes**: lane width is one constant (`LANE_W`, 3.6 m in `src/00_util.js`); `laneInfo(hw,style)` gives lanes per direction from the road width, and both the road shader (painted dashed lines, centre line / median) and the traffic lane graph use it, so the paint and the AI lanes always agree. Markings fade towards junctions.
* **Lane graph** (`src/23_lanes.js`, built once at boot in about 0.1 s, 482 lane edges, 2,377 turn links, 434 lane-km, 2 MB): one centre-line per lane per direction for every road except the patches (Lombard Street, the pier), stored in travel order. Junctions are found where a road end touches another road (T, ramp, end to end, three roads at one point) and where two roads cross at the same level; a turn is a short cubic curve from one lane to another (right turns from the outer lane, left turns from the inner lane, a lane that runs out of road may turn from any lane; no left turns across a highway median, so highways get a U-turn crossover about every 500 m; dead ends get a U-turn; a curve that would be shorter than 8 m is pushed further out). Each lane point has a comfortable speed from the road type, curvature and grade. `node racer/tests/lanecheck.js` checks it (the road directions form one strongly connected piece, so a car can never get trapped).
* **Traffic** (`src/60_traffic.js` simulation, `src/61_trafficgl.js` cars): up to 56 cars (`?tmax=N` changes it, `?traffic=0` turns it off) spawned out of sight 140-430 m from the player and removed beyond 540 m (or behind the player past 300 m); more cars in town (downtown 56, the Valley 51, towns 37, highways about 22-27, mountain roads about 19-22). Each car has a horizon (12 points, 6-10 m apart, 66-110 m ahead) along its lane and the turn it picked at the next junction (a seeded hash, so it never changes its mind); IDM car following with a speed profile from the lane speeds ahead; it gives way by comparing its rectangle with every other car's rectangle at the same moment over the next 4 s (road class and turn type decide who goes, then who is nearer; a car that has given way keeps doing so for a second; anything about to touch while still moving brakes whatever the priority); it changes lane to get into the lane of its turn, and to get past a slow car; swerves and brakes for the player; steers by pure pursuit (a kinematic bicycle). A car that has been waiting for a few seconds goes anyway, and a jam that lasts 12-22 s away from the player is dissolved. Five body types (sedan, hatch, SUV, van, police car) in 12 colours with head and tail lights (brake lights light up), drawn instanced with two levels of detail: cars within 52 m of the camera (34 m on Low) use a **smooth body made by the same generator as the player's cars** (about 2.8k triangles: rounded flanks, shoulders and roof, glass, wheels with rims, no facets), the rest the old 200-triangle body; one draw per type and level (up to 10 draws). Everything is deterministic (one seeded generator, fixed 60 Hz tick shared with the 120 Hz player step).
* **Crashes**: a hit is a real collision between two rectangles (mass 1.1-2.3 t, restitution .26, friction .32) with an impulse at the contact point, so a hit car spins. A light bump just slows the car; above 2.5 kJ it becomes a wreck (a free body: slides, spins, stops at walls and trees, stays up to 30 s and goes away sooner once out of sight). Every impact is recorded in `IMP` (`40_car.js`): source (0 building/tree/pole, 1 steep bank, 2 landing, 3 traffic), closing speed, **energy lost in joules**, contact point and normal, the car's zone (0 front, 1 rear, 2 left, 3 right), the other mass. The damage system reads `IMP`.
* **Damage** (`src/62_damage.js`): every impact in `IMP` adds its energy to one of five zones (front, rear, left, right, roof; a zone is destroyed by `DZ_CAP` = 650 / 520 / 420 / 420 / 320 kJ, a sedan at 24 m/s into a parked car is about 180 kJ; 14 % of a hit spills into the neighbouring zones; scrapes below 3.5 m/s or 8 kJ and landings below 11 m/s do nothing). The zones feed **one health bar** (HEALTH, above the NITRO bar) and decide where the dents and the engine smoke go. **Damage never changes how the car drives** (`DMG.spd/acc` stay 1, `pull/leak` 0): it is health and looks only; the car is wrecked when the front or rear is gone or health is under 16 %. **Full dents**: the real vertices of the car body near the contact are pushed in along the direction of the hit (Gaussian of the pristine position, up to 0.34 m deep, never more than 0.5 m from where they were, noise a function of position so no cracks open), normals are shaken so the dent catches the light and the paint scuffs; the body buffer is re-uploaded with `bufferSubData` (at once for a big hit, at most every 0.2 s for a run of small ones). Repair puts the pristine copy back. **Traffic cars are dented too**: each car keeps two dents (centre on its body, depth, radius) from its crashes (`trDent` in `60_traffic.js`); the traffic vertex shader pushes the metal in towards the middle of the car, darkens and dulls it there.
* **Safe houses** (`src/63_safe.js`): the garages on the map each have a glowing pad in front of the roller door (green = yours, red = locked). Park on it (under 9 m/s, 1.5 s, the pad slows you) and the car is repaired, the heat is cleared and the spot is saved. The Home Garage near the start is free and owned from the first second; the others are locked. **The money system is NOT decided**: `ECON` is a set of hooks that do nothing (`price(id)` returns null, `canBuy/buy` return false, `repairCost` is 0, `onBusted/onWrecked/onRepair/onUnlock` are empty) and `SAFE.price[id]` is null for every house but Home. Call `safeUnlock(id)` once a house has been paid for; progress is kept in `localStorage` (`racer.save`). Wrecked or busted: a 3.4 s end screen, then you respawn on the pad of the nearest garage you own, repaired and with no cops. `?safe=all` unlocks every garage for testing.
* **Cops** (`src/64_cops.js`, cars from `60_traffic.js` with body type 4): heat 1-5 (stars), earned from crimes (hitting traffic, wrecking cars, hitting cops, speeding past a cop) and kept by being seen. Difficulty presets in `COPD` (`?cops=off|medium|hard`; **Forgiving is the default**): Forgiving cops top out at 46 m/s, send 0/1/2/2/3/4 chasers at heat 0-5, only ram from heat 5, set up roadblocks (with a 6.5 m gap) at heat 5, need 5 s boxed in at under 6 m/s to bust you and lose you after 8 s out of sight. Medium (56 m/s, rams from heat 3, roadblocks from 4, bust in 3 s) and Hard (66 m/s, rams from 2, roadblocks from 3, bust in 2 s) are there but not tuned by hand. Cops spawn out of sight (patrols anywhere, chasers 150-270 m behind) and, when idle, patrol along the lane graph with lights off; a patrol that sees you commit a crime starts a chase. Chasers pick lanes greedily towards you, overtake slower cars, switch to free driving (steering at a slot beside, behind or in front of you) once close, and try to box you in; a cop that is hit too hard is wrecked. Escape: break line of sight (range 300 m) for 8 s, outrun them, or park in an owned garage. Sirens (instance flag, red/blue light bar materials, a bold plain-blended red/blue glow that shows even against the bright sky, a coloured wash on the road, a two-tone oscillator), **patrol cars flash their light bar slowly even with the siren off** so you can pick them out, more patrols (3 / 4 / 5 on Forgiving / Medium / Hard) and 70 % of them spawn ahead of you; while you are wanted, **edge markers show where the chasers are** when you cannot see them (a flashing arrow along the top edge, left / right / behind, with the distance), the stars and bust meter on the HUD, flashing dots on the minimap.
* **Look** (a gloomy, overcast DAY; the story is not written, the mood is just there): the sun is a pale patch behind cloud (`ENV` in `src/30_render.js`: weak, high, grey), the light comes from the whole sky, the horizon is thick grey haze (fog density 1/1150 m, thinning with height) and the haze colour is the sky's horizon colour so distance melts into it. The grade (`tone()` in `src/10_gl.js`) is a filmic curve, desaturated to 72 %, cool in the shadows, a touch of contrast. The sky has two cloud layers moving at different speeds (a third octave on the higher tiers), darker where thick, a diffuse bright patch where the sun is. **Roads are wet**: darker asphalt, puddles (a noise field) that act like mirrors, fine ripples, a fresnel reflection of the sky, and a pool of light plus a long view-aligned reflection streak for each of the lights near the car (`uLt`: the car's headlight pool and tail light, the nearest street lamps, the nearest traffic lights; 4 / 6 / 8 on Low / Medium / High). Street lamps are lit (emissive heads, a glow sprite and a pool of light on the road), about a quarter of the windows are lit (a hash per window, warm or cool), downtown and the Valley have 125 neon signs (emissive boxes + glow sprites that flicker), cars have glowing head and tail lights (positions read from the baked mesh's emissive material) and headlight beams on the road, police cars have flashing glow on the siren. Smoke is soft noisy puffs, there are sparks from every crash (`IMP`), and tyre spray off the wet road at speed.
* **Car shader**: the baked garage mesh (it already is the high-detail bake: 9,474 triangles for the body, 1,768 and 1,876 per wheel pair) gets a fake environment (dark wet ground, bright hazy horizon, overcast dome, a soft patch where the sun is, a long overhead softbox for the clear-coat highlight line), a second clear-coat reflection, metallic flakes, road dirt up the lower body, brake lights that glow harder on the brakes. Damage dents are still real vertex displacement (see Damage).
* **Graphics tiers and post pass** (`src/31_gfx.js`, `src/32_shadow.js`, `src/41_fx.js`): the scene is drawn into an off-screen target at the render scale and one pass puts it on the canvas: **FXAA** always, **speed blur** (radial, from about 45 m/s, more with nitro, the car stays sharp) and **bloom** (bright parts at quarter size, two blur passes, added back) on the higher tiers, plus a vignette. **Low**: render scale 0.55-0.85, no shadow map (the blob shadow), 4 wet-road lights, up to 26 lamp glows. **Medium**: scale 0.6-0.95, speed blur, a 512 x 512 sun shadow map over 68 m around the car (the car and the traffic cast), 6 lights, 48 glows. **High**: scale 0.7-1.0, bloom, a 1024 x 1024 map over 92 m that also takes the buildings, trees and lamp posts, 8 lights, 80 glows, 1.25 x the traffic. The shadows are soft and weak (the sun is behind cloud): a 2 x 2 or 3 x 3 filter, strength 0.5, the grid is anchored to the world so nothing shimmers. **Auto / High / Low** (the GFX button, or `?q=auto|high|low`, remembered in `localStorage`; `?tier=0|1|2` pins a tier for testing): in Auto the tier is chosen from MEASURED frame cost, never from the phone model. A frame rate capped at the screen rate says nothing about how much room is left, so every 2.6 s a probe times 8 frames with a forced wait for the GPU (a 1-pixel readback) and the median is the real cost of a frame (CPU + GPU, not overlapped, so a bit pessimistic). Rules that keep a phone from running flat out: step DOWN at 13.5 ms or when frames arrive late (resolution first, then the tier; a tier that failed is locked out for 70 s); step UP only if the estimated cost one step up stays under 9.5 ms (57 % of a 60 fps frame) twice in a row; between 9.5 and 13.5 nothing changes. Auto starts on Medium. The numbers (9.5, 13.5, the tier cost ratios 1 : 1.35 : 1.9) are design values, **not measured on a phone**.
* **Rendering**: terrain is a GPU clip-map (6 rings, one draw each) displaced from a 16-bit height texture; roads are ribbons with analytic
  lane markings; buildings/trees/barriers are merged per 128 m cell and culled by distance + frustum (the haze lets the draw distances be shorter: roads 1.9 km, buildings 1.25 km on Medium); sky, fog and water are shaders.
  About 50-140 draw calls and 75-115k triangles per frame depending on tier and place. No reflections of buildings in the road (the sky and the lights are reflected).
* **Cars** (`src/70_carmesh.js` the generator, `src/71_cars.js` install, `src/72_roster.js` the 30 cars, `src/51_garage.js` the menu): there are **30 made-up supercars**, none a real car and none with a real name, each in the spirit of a real one (a rear-engine coupe like a 911, mid-engine V8 and V10 wedges, front-engine V12 grand tourers, American muscle, lightweight British carbon cars, Japanese all-wheel-drive and hybrid cars, hypercars). Every body is made at start-up from a few numbers by one generator (about 25 ms each): a loft of smooth cross-sections (underbody, flanks, shoulder, bonnet and deck, with a smooth normal everywhere, so no flat polygon facets), wheel wells that slope into the body, a glasshouse (windscreen, side windows with slanted pillar edges, roof, rear glass or engine cover), headlights (round, oval, slit, LED, pop-up), tail lights (bar, wide bar, round, quad, slit), intakes, vents, door lines, mirrors, bonnet duct, engine-cover louvres, diffuser, exhaust tips, wings (lip, ducktail, GT wing, big wing) and stripes, plus real wheels (tyre profile, rim barrel, 5-14 spokes or a mesh or a disc, brake disc and caliper) that spin and steer. 5-10k triangles per car, 15 materials (paint takes the chosen colour, glass, trim, carbon, chrome, emissive lights, rubber, rim, caliper, exhaust). The first car, **Kestrel S**, is the rear-engine coupe you start with. **Cars are drawn with the same dent engine as before** (see Damage). The `CARS` button (or `C`) opens the **car menu**: the world stands still and the camera circles the car, with the car's figures as bars (top speed, acceleration, grip, brakes, lightness), 8 paint colours, arrows and a map of dots to browse all 30. **How cars are earned is not decided** (like the money system), so **for now all 30 cars are unlocked** (`CAR_ALL_DEFAULT` in `src/51_garage.js`; the menu's `UNLOCK ALL CARS` switch turns it off to see the locked state). The earning system will set it to false and call `carUnlock(id)` (the hook) as cars are earned; only the first car is owned without it, `ECON.carPrice(id)` is null and a locked car says "How to earn this car is not decided yet". `?car=N` starts in car N. What you own, drive and the paint are saved (`localStorage`).
* **Handling per car** (`carHandling` in `src/72_roster.js`): each car carries the figures of the kind of car it is inspired by (power, mass, which wheels drive, top speed, 0-100 km/h, best lateral g, braking distance from 100 km/h) and they are turned into the numbers of the driving model (`HND` in `40_car.js`) relative to the first car, which keeps exactly the old numbers: top speed (compressed, 75 % of the difference, so a 440 km/h car is not twice as fast as a 308 km/h one), acceleration, cornering grip (and a little more aero grip for the cars with downforce), brakes, agility (lighter and shorter turns in faster), and the drive layout: all-wheel drive grips a little more and slides less, rear-wheel-drive front- and mid-engine cars break traction under throttle at low speed the more power they have for their weight (`HND.powerOS`), mid-engine cars turn in sharper and let go sooner. `node racer/tests/rcars.js` measures all 30 on flat ground and compares the order with the real figures (rank correlation 0.97-1.0 for top speed, 0-100, braking and grip). It is an arcade model: the figures decide the character, they are not a simulation of those cars.
* **Driving** (`HND` constants at the top of `src/40_car.js`): grip first, like the Need for Speed arcade games. Steering is limited by a tyre budget
  (about 1.4-2.3 g), so at speed full lock runs wide instead of sliding. Speed is shown in mph. Tuned like a sports car: firm brakes (60 mph to a stop in about 1.2 s) and quick turn-in.
  **Drift pedal**: a *tap* kicks the tail out (a slide of about 15-25 degrees towards the way you steer, or the side you last steered when the stick is centred) that carries on for about
  half a second after the finger lifts, then a stability assist straightens the car; the stick picks a bigger slide angle. The *longer you hold* it the harder it brakes (a light drag at first, the full 24 m/s^2
  locked-wheel brake after 1.2 s), and the slide angle the stick can ask for grows as the car slows, so a full-stick hold turns the car right round (about 170 degrees in 1.2 s at 60 mph, 2 s at 100, 2.9 s at 140).
  **Any slide is a drift**: points, nitro, tyre smoke and skid marks come from the slide angle (more than about 8 degrees at over 22 mph), not from the pedal, so a slide that carries on after a tap, after a landing or on grass counts too;
  a half-second grace keeps the combo alive across a change of direction. The DRIFT and AIR / JUMP readouts are small, count up live, stay about two seconds after the move ends, then float up and fade out.
  **Energy**: everything is per unit mass, with speed measured along the ground. Gravity pulls the car along the slope exactly (`a = -g m/(1+m^2)`, also sideways down a camber), so climbing turns speed into height
  and descending turns height into speed (coasting over a 30 m hill loses exactly `g h` plus drag; the test sums kinetic + potential + drag work to within 4 %). Brakes, the drift and bumps turn kinetic energy into heat.
  **Air**: the car follows the ground only while the ground pushes up on it. Over a crest of vertical curvature k the ground needs a downward acceleration of `k v^2`; once that is more than gravity the tyres lose the road and
  the car flies on ballistically with the velocity it had (energy conserved, only a little air drag; the engine revs freely and the nose follows the arc). It lands as soon as it meets the ground again: the part of the velocity that points into the ground is
  absorbed (that energy, half the normal speed squared, is lost: shake, camera bob and a small extra scrub above 12 m/s), the rest is kept, so a flat landing keeps all its horizontal speed, a landing on a down slope turns the fall into
  forward speed and one on an up slope loses some. Crossing a crest of height h and length L launches a car above about `sqrt(g L^2 / (2 pi^2 h))`; there are three groups of *jump hills* (6 m, 68 m crests, about 68 mph to take off,
  a 1 s / 50 m flight at 100 mph) on the ring's south straight, the southern highway (two groups) and the east link, marked JUMPS on the map. Also off any bank or bridge edge, and over real hills on the mountain roads.
  Cars stay on the road over small bumps, the body leans and dives. **The camera follows the car as it is DRAWN** (interpolated between the 120 Hz physics steps; before, the car jittered by up to 0.7 m against the camera at speed), the boost eases in and out (about 0.15 s / 0.3 s: push, top speed, camera pull-back and field of view all follow one number, `C.boostK`), the camera's turning is a critically damped spring and the drift look-ahead eases in over a third of a second (`rcam.js` measures it). Nitro is filled by drifting and is a latch: one tap starts it, it runs until the tank is empty (tap again or brake to stop). Building, barrier and bank collisions, respawn.
* **Controls**: touch (drag the left half to steer; GAS and BRAKE pedals bottom right, DRIFT above brake, NITRO above gas; tap the minimap (top left) for the world map;
  AUTO = automatic gas) or keyboard (arrows/WASD, Space drift, Shift nitro, R respawn, M map, G auto-gas, F fps, P pause).

`21_terrain` height map/carving/ground colours, `22_build` static geometry + spatial queries, `23_lanes` lane graph, `30_render` frame, `40_car` car/physics/camera/effects/impacts,
`50_ui` input/HUD/minimap/audio/quality, `31_gfx` tiers + post pass + lights near the car + the automatic quality choice, `32_shadow` sun shadows, `41_fx` shadow blob, skid marks, smoke, spray, sparks, glows, beams, `51_garage` the car menu, `60_traffic` traffic simulation, `61_trafficgl` traffic cars (smooth LOD + dents), `70_carmesh` the car generator, `71_cars` installing a car, `72_roster` the 30 cars + handling, `62_damage` damage + dents, `63_safe` safe houses + economy stub + wrecked/busted, `64_cops` cops and heat, `90_main` boot + loop (`worldStep` = player at 120 Hz + traffic at 60 Hz).
