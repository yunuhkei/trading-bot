HOW TO PLAY

👆

Steer

Touch the left side of the screen to turn left and the right side to turn right.

⏳

Stand & leap

Hold STAND to slow time, then tap a side to jump that way. Let go to climb back in.

⚠️

Don't slip

Landing on a car isn't always safe. You can slip off, more often when its speed is very different from yours.

🤝

Second chance

If you slip, you grab the nearest car if one is close. Otherwise it's over.

🚓

Cops

They ram and they shoot. Keep moving.
